A few good questions

A welcome
with clarity.

Already have an account? Log in to your workspace.

Can I access opportunities without registering a business?

Yes. Choose Contracting opportunities only. A business name is not required to create that account. You can add company facts later for company-based proposals or partnership publication.

Does the platform submit bids for me?

No. It helps you prepare, edit and approve drafts. Actual submission follows the current solicitation’s designated instructions. You can record a submission reference after you submit externally.

Will every attachment be fully readable?

Source restrictions, unsupported formats, scanned documents and processing limits can prevent full extraction. Gaps are shown explicitly. Summaries, analysis and proposal drafting are blocked until the current notice, description and every directly linked attachment have been fully extracted. Historical versions are not independently retrieved.

What is shared when I publish a partnership listing?

Only the company information you choose to publish in the member directory. Private profile contact fields and proposal documents are not copied automatically. A company must accept an invitation before private messaging starts.

How do paid recommendations and drafting work?

Review the maximum price and data-sharing consent before paid processing. Your workspace balance funds approved requests. No paid request is initiated by watching this demo or browsing this homepage.

Is this a government service or a promise of an award?

No. This is an independent workspace using configured source integrations. SAM.gov is an official U.S. government source. Search results, company listings and generated recommendations do not verify eligibility, credentials or an award.
